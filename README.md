## 🎛️ What tapline Does

## 🔊 Per-App Mixer for Everyday Control

The per-app mixer lets you set different volume levels for different applications. This is useful when a meeting app is quieter than your music player or when a video app is louder than everything else. Instead of changing the volume in every app, you can balance them from one place.

A simple approach is to set your main listening app at a comfortable level, then lower or raise other apps until they sit naturally in the mix. Keep the changes modest at first. A small adjustment is usually easier to live with than a large one.

## ⚙️ System-Wide Parametric EQ

The parametric EQ lets you adjust specific frequency ranges across your system audio. You can lift the bass for a warmer sound, soften the treble for long listening sessions, or bring forward the vocal range for clearer dialogue. Each band can be adjusted without affecting the rest of your setup.

Start with one or two bands. Make a small change, listen for a moment, and then decide whether to keep it. If the sound becomes harsh or muddy, return the band toward the center and try a smaller adjustment. Presets are helpful when you want a quick starting point.
